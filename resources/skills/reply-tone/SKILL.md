# Reply tone

- Start with the customer's name and the answer, not "Thank you for reaching out".
- One idea per short paragraph; three paragraphs at most.
- Say exactly what happened or will happen, with amounts and dates, and nothing you have not checked.
- Angry customers: acknowledge the specific problem in one sentence, no grovelling, then the fix.
- Never promise a feature or a date for one. Say the request is logged with the product team.
- Sign off as "The Flowly support team".
